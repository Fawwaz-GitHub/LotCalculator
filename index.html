Calculation Formula
LOT Total Amount = Total Lot Count × Lot Per Piece Price

Section Amount = Section Piece Count × Section Per Piece Rate

Calculated Rate = (LOT Total Amount − All Completed Section Amounts) ÷ Sum of Percentage Below
Example with 100 lots and lot rate 45:
LOT: 100 × 45 = 4500
Section 1: 25 × 55 = 1375
Section 1 Rate: (4500 − 1375) ÷ 75 = 41.67
Section 2: 20 × 44 = 880
Section 2 Rate: (4500 − 1375 − 880) ÷ 55 = 40.82
